🩸 Smart Blood Donor Management System

About Smart Blood Donor

Smart Blood Donor Management System is a web-based platform designed to connect blood donors with people who need blood.

Users can search for available donors based on blood group and city. The main aim of this system is to make finding a suitable blood donor faster, easier and more efficient during emergencies.

The system stores donor information securely in a MySQL database and allows users to search donor details quickly.
